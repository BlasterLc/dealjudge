# Product

## Register
product

## Users
A Chilean shopper during a Cyber event, on a laptop or a phone, in a hurry and suspicious of "discounts" that were inflated a few weeks earlier. They want one answer per product: does this price deserve my attention, yes or no, and how sure is that answer.

## Product purpose
Dealjudge watches specific products, judges with Strands Decider 2B whether a discount is real against the 90-day price history, and says so in plain Spanish with the figures that back it. It speaks up only when there is something real to say, or when it has doubts, and then it says so.

## Brand personality
Honest, dry, a little stubborn. A shop receipt that does the maths for you. No hype, no exclamation marks, no assistant voice.

## Anti-references
Chat bubbles and gradients that mark a page as "AI". Dashboards of identical metric cards. Neon on dark. Stock-photo shoppers.

## Design principles
1. The figures are the interface: price, median and the chart carry the page; copy only connects them.
2. A verdict is a stamp, not a decoration: one clear action word per product, always with the reason beside it.
3. Doubt is shown, never hidden: a warning reads as plain as an alert.
4. Everything shown here is simulated, and the page says so.

## Accessibility & inclusion
WCAG AA contrast on the paper palette. Fully keyboard operable. Spanish (Chile) copy; tabular numerals for every price.
